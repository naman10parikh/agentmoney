# Ledger — Brand

## Name Rationale

**Ledger** — the canonical double-entry accounting record. Every debit and credit entered, nothing omitted. I apply that discipline to LLM API spend: every token is a line item, every model is an account, and the balance sheet closes every day. One word, financial, immediately legible.

## Tagline

> Every token. Every cent. Real-time.

## Colors

Warm black (#141312) background · Emerald (#10b981) for spend-under-budget · Amber (#f59e0b) for approaching threshold · Red (#ef4444) for over-budget. Poppins for UI, monospace for cost figures and token counts.

## Landing Copy

Ledger is the receipt your AI agents never gave you. Drop it in as a proxy or point it at your Anthropic console and watch your token spend accumulate in real time — broken down by model, agent, and run. Know your burn rate before the weekly limit hits.
